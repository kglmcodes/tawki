# Zhuyin (注音符號 / Bopomofo)

Taiwan's phonetic system for Mandarin. It is a *sound notation*, not a separate
writing system — the writing system is still Hanzi; Zhuyin sits beside the
characters the way pinyin does in the mainland. Taiwanese children learn to
read with it, and it is what Taiwan keyboards type.

37 symbols: 21 initials, 16 finals, plus 5 tone marks. A syllable is written
initial + final, with the tone mark added.

|---|---|---|
| ˉ | first | high and flat (usually written with no mark) |

### Notes

- **Buzzing vowel:** after ㄓㄔㄕㄖㄗㄘㄙ the vowel is written as part of the
  initial — 知 is just ㄓ, 吃 is ㄔ, 四 is ㄙ (no ㄧ shown).
- **j/q/x + ㄩ:** pinyin writes `-u` (ju, qu, xu) but the sound is ü: ㄐㄩ, ㄑㄩ,
  ㄒㄩ.
- **Neutral tone** goes in front: 的 = ˙ㄉㄜ, 謝謝 = ㄒㄧㄝˋ ˙ㄒㄧㄝ.
